## Finding
The screenshot (CleanShot 2026-11-02 at 00.02.55@2x.png, 1592x158) does not show the Mac desktop UI.
Evidence:
- Rendering plugins/ui/desktop/desktop_ui.c with the existing mem backend (build/sdl selecta, --canvas 796x79 and
  1592x158 --scale 2, --frame-dump) gives a different bar: round transport buttons without tile fills, no heart,
  no stop button, no right group of chevrons, no "Stopped" text.
- The screenshot matches plugins/ui/mui/mui_ui.c mui_player_group (line ~732): a four-row Group in one panel:
  row1 transport buttons, row2 album/speed extras row (mui_p8_extras_row), then the seek row (clock, seek slider,
  remaining, length, volume slider) and row3 (title, artist, format, "Stopped" status text, mui_ui.c:763).
  The "Stopped" label, the 00:00 / -03:13 / 03:13 clock labels and the tile-filled buttons are MUI texts and
  mui_aabtn buttons. The shot is a ~79 px tall window, so MUI squeezes four rows into one band, which explains the
  rows not sharing one centre line, unequal button widths (each button sized from its own label) and the
  clipped cover.
- The Mac dui layout helpers named in the brief (footer_y0, seek_bar_rect, desk_button_rect) are not involved.
