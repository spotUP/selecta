## I963 static findings (unproven)

mui_ui.c asks MUIA_Window_Height MUIV_Window_Height_Screen(100) with MUIA_Window_ID set and no TopEdge. Candidates: a saved MUI snapshot
restored through MUIA_Window_ID (ENV:MUI), Workbench title/border limits, or a bounded max height in the PageMode main group or sidebar.
Needs the rig window geometry (uitree W line, ami.py screens) and a check with the ENV:MUI snapshot removed.
