# I1198: album titles starve on the Mac desktop collection page

Invariant: the page's song set is its identity. pls_reload_run kept nothing across reloads: every library_serial change (Recents touch of the open page, play counts, playlist creates) reset titles to file names, bumped pls_ver and re-posted the briefs read; pls_briefs_follow drops answers whose brf_ver != pls_ver, so a stream of touches starved the read.
