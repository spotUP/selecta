# I1248 close button on the fullscreen visualizer

Johan (Mac, 2026-11-10): no way to leave fullscreen visuals; wants a close button upper right, visible when hovering.

## Changed
- plugins/ui/desktop/desktop_ui.c: viz_close_draw paints a round close button (X icon, SURFACE_RAISED, SURFACE_HOVER when hot) in the upper right of the visual layout. A pointer move shows it; it hides after 2.5 s idle (same timer pattern as the toast). New control CTL_VIZ_CLOSE with tooltip. A primary click runs the layout_full action line (PREF_SET ui.desktop.mode full). --stats gained a viz_close line.
- src/uimodel/actions.c, include/selecta/uimodel.h: sel_uim_tip_viz_close() = "Close Visualizer" (shared string).
- tests/dui/viz_close_button.cmake (dui-viz-close): hidden at rest, drawn on a move, lit on hover, hidden when idle, click returns to layout 0.
- tests/dui/viz_hover_behind.cmake: frame is now captured after the idle hide (q@4200) because the button legitimately appears on a move.

## Proof
- dui-viz-close, dui-viz-hover-behind, dui-viz-tip-flicker, dui-hover-regions pass.
- Revert check: click branch disabled gives "a click on the close button: layout 3, want 0 (full)"; restored, passes.
