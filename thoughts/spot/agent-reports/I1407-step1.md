# I1407 step 1: shared UHC client, AmigaRemix and Remix.Kwed.org sources

## What changed
- plugins/shared/source/uhc.{h,c}, uhc_parse.c: one UHC client (`sel_uhc_search`, `sel_uhc_parse`) with a searchtype parameter, moved out of modulespl. Modules.pl now calls it through `sel_modulespl_uhc_row`.
- plugins/shared/source/uhc_remix.{h,c}: row to key `listen/<id>/<file>.mp3`, parse hook, stream URL (percent-encoded). Rejects non-listen rows, non-mp3 names, `..`, control characters and `| " \ ? #`.
- plugins/sources/amigaremix and plugins/sources/rko: thin SOURCE_SEARCH plugins, flags REMOTE | STREAMS, play_url from `https://uhc.amigaremix.com/` and `https://uhc.remix.kwed.org/` (bases from HippoPlayer.js UhcSearches). Env overrides SELECTA_AMIGAREMIX_UHC, SELECTA_RKO_UHC.
- tests: `uhc-remix-parse` (new, fixtures amigaremixsearch_turrican.json, rkosearch_turrican.json recorded from the live index), `modulespl-parse` still built from the shared parser; tests/m3/sources.expected and sources_off.expected list the two new sources.

## Terms of use (read 2026-11-15)
- AmigaRemix FAQ (https://amigaremix.com/faq): music may be used "for your own personal leisure" on your own devices. Any commercial use, public redistribution or ad monetisation needs the remix artist's and original composer's consent. Attribution expected. Bulk download allowed with own tools but fast mass download may get you blocked. Streaming to a user's own player = personal use; Selecta must not mirror or redistribute (it does not cache: STREAMS).
- Remix.Kwed.org (front page small print): files "assumed to be freely distributable"; removal on request of a rights holder; downloader holds the site harmless. No separate ToS text found for listening (a "Terms of Service" link is on the upload flow, not read). Privacy policy is cookie-only.
- Verdict: both fine for search plus personal streaming. Do not bundle or mirror audio. Show attribution (artist column is filled from the index).
