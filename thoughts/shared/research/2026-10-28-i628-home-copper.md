# I628: Home covers and background not on the AGA copper

## Observation (Johan, rig 2, AGA desktop UI, Home)

"the covers dont use copper colors now" and "the home bg is not a copperbar either".

## Step 1: regression?

No. The behaviour dates from I441 (85d3c6cc), when the copper got 4 pens and 4 keys.
Each distinct gradient key (ry, rh, a, b) took its own pen for the whole screen:

- The 300 lpx Home header band (`HOME_TINT_H`, `draw_home_header`) took pen 255.
- The first 3 genart covers took pens 254-252.
- Every later cover fell back to the dither (`gradient_raw`).
- The page below the 300 lpx band was plain panel, never a copper bar.

No later commit changed how genart covers get pens (gui_genart.c has no copper change).
Copper commits checked: 85d3c6cc, 1baa425e, 959122cd, 8f56e1c4, 3032233f, 40a3693d,
0a971b5f (I595), 7576d82e (log lines), 6174a4b6 (I600).

`copper_n=1` in err.log is logged only on the first present (`cop_logged`), so it says
nothing about Home.

Host measurement on the old allocation: `copper keys=4 miss=0 changes=301`, with three
cover keys on the same rows (110/72 three times). With 4 covers plus the background,
at least one cover dithered; the old code had no miss counter to show it.

## Root cause

Pen allocation was per gradient, not per row band. Pens are a per-row resource on the
copper: two gradients on disjoint rows can share one pen. The allocator spent one pen per
gradient for the whole screen, so 4 pens ran out after the header plus 3 covers.

## Fix (3db46bb6)

- `gui_draw.h`: `SEL_GUI_COPPER_KEYS 16`; canvas `copper_mask` and `copper_miss`;
  key carries its `pen`.
- `gui_draw.c`: a gradient takes the first pen free on its rows (`copper_pick`);
  same rows share a key (I510); on failure a GC drops keys whose pen no longer appears
  in their rows (`copper_collect`), then dithers and counts `copper_miss`.
  `sel_gui_copper_changes` emits each key's pen.
- `desktop_ui.c`: on a copper canvas `draw_home_header` draws the gradient over the full
  main region (HEADER_TINT to PANEL). RGBA keeps the 300 lpx band, so goldens are unchanged.
  New `--copper N` option; `home_dump_build --stats` prints `copper pens keys miss changes`
  and one `copper_key` line per key.
