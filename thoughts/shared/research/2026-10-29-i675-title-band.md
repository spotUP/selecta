# I675: what drew the band at the top of the SELECTA screen

## Cause

The main window's own Intuition title bar. Intuition gives a window a title bar whenever it has a title (or system
gadgets), even with WFLG_BORDERLESS. mui_open passed `MUIA_Window_Title "Selecta"` to the borderless backdrop window, so
Intuition reserved BorderTop = font height + 1 = 9 rows and drew the title "Selecta" in them (rows 0-8 of Johan's grab).
The screen title bar was not involved: SA_ShowTitle FALSE (a23b7368) had already cleared SHOWTITLE, which is why that fix
changed nothing.

## Evidence (rig 2, live structs read by a probe: LockPubScreen("SELECTA"), walk FirstWindow)

Before (binary from 07dae701):

    screen 640x512 Flags=0602 SHOWTITLE=0 BarHeight=10 BarVBorder=1 WBorTop=2 Font=8 Title=Selecta
    win 'Selecta' st='Selecta' 0,0 640x512 Bor L0 T9 R0 B0 Flags=0c203b20 BACKDROP=1 BORDERLESS=1 inner 640x503

After (aa3cf8f9):

    screen 640x512 Flags=0602 SHOWTITLE=0 ...
    win '(null)' st='Selecta' 0,0 640x512 Bor L0 T0 R0 B0 Flags=0c203b20 BACKDROP=1 BORDERLESS=1 inner 640x512

Pixel rows after the fix (grab 640x512, most common colours per row): rows 0-2 all (18,18,20); from row 3 the UI
(sidebar (30,30,36) 160 px, frame (44,44,52)). No "Selecta" text in the top rows. The 3 plain rows are MUI's window
inner spacing, not a bar.

## Fix

`mui_window_attrs_t` gains `titled` (0 on the own screen, 1 on Workbench); mui_open passes
`MUIA_Window_Title, wattr.titled ? "Selecta" : NULL`. SA_ShowTitle FALSE stays (correct: the screen bar stays behind the
backdrop window). Help and Settings windows keep their titles.

## Test

ctest `mui-window-borderless` (tests/ui/mui_contract_test.c test_window_borderless) checks titled == 0 on the own screen,
1 on Workbench, and that the WindowObject takes its title from it. With mui_ui.c and mui_screen.c reverted it fails on
both checks (lines 1037, 1055). Full `-L mui -E rig-`: 81/82 pass; `mui-weights` fails at mui_contract_test.c:333
(sidebar weight vs region at width 800/640), unrelated to this change, not checked against main.
