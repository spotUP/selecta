## Mutation checks done

- no-alloc: one allocation per pump block turned the test red (9 allocations); source restored.
- amiga-roundtrip: pointing the driver at the arm64 golden turned it red.
- registry-tags-negative: a bad plugin with tag 0x2FFF must give `unknown_tags=1` and a non-zero exit.

## Deviations from the plan

- no-alloc runs on macOS only (dyld interposing). Linux CI does not cover it.
- The marker lines are caught by interposing fputs and fwrite; interposing write does not reach libc's inner write.
- libnix clock() crashed every subcommand under vamos and was replaced earlier in M0a.
- The x86_64 abi golden is hand-derived and unverified until the ubuntu CI job runs.
- The Amiga CI job is not added; it needs a verified bebbo toolchain image (TODO I21 in ci.yml).
