# M0b validation

## T15 nuade-ab

`tests/nuade_ab/nuade_ab` renders `selecta-test.mod` (10 s, 44100 Hz) through stock
nuade (`nuade_song.c` + `nuade_mixer.c`, float, library `nuade_stock`, only
`protracker4`) and compares it with the WAV from `selecta-cli render --resample linear`
(the ctest drives the CLI, then the executable reads its WAV; not in-process).
Method of DEViLBOX `nuadeCompare.ts`: 20 ms RMS envelopes of the mono sum, Pearson
correlation in double, lag 0. Threshold: `C >= 0.98`, `W == 500`.

Measured (host, macOS arm64): `windows=500 correlation=0.9987` (linear, the gated
case). Informational, same song: none 0.9996, cubic 0.9993, sinc 0.9993. No defect
found in the mixer, `voices.c` or `voices_shim.c`.
